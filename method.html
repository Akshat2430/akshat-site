90/10 Method

I'm an independent strategist who builds the function that doesn't exist yet. Give me a gap and I'll build what should be sitting in it.

Every engagement runs the same three moves.

01 · Map the gap

Start with the decision. I work with leaders, customers, and the people closest to the work to separate the real opportunity from the stated brief.

02 · Prove it small

Build the lightest version that can create evidence: a pilot, market map, operating cadence, decision system, GTM playbook, or first team. The point is to replace opinion with a useful result and learn before the organisation makes the expensive move.

03 · Scale what works

Turn proof into a capability. I stay close enough to make the system work, then step back when it can run without me.

Got a bet with no owner?

You've got the ambition. I'll build what needs to sit behind it.

02 / How bets stall

Most big bets don't fail.
They quietly stall.

Where it stallsWhat I build

  1. The mandate stays fuzzy.

    Everyone agrees it matters. Nobody agrees what it is.

    → Shape the bet

    Market maps, customer truth, opportunity sizing, and a clear answer to where to play and how to win.

  2. The thing it needs doesn't exist.

    No function, no system, no route to market. Just a very good deck.

    → Build the function

    First operating model, intelligence engine, GTM system, or strategy and analytics capability, from scratch.

  3. It works until the champion leaves.

    The pilot glows. Then someone changes roles and it quietly dies.

    → Make it hold

    Pilots, governance, measurement, and ownership until it runs without me.

Every quarter a bet stays “promising” is a quarter someone else spends building it.

Temporary moonshot. Lasting business capability.

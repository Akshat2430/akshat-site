What this actually needs

Nobody lit this candle on purpose. It shows up when a pilot launches with hope instead of a plan: no owner, no baseline, no check-in booked before the excitement fades. Hope isn't a rollout strategy, and a launch-week spike isn't adoption.

Swap it for

A named owner, a one-week check-in on the calendar before launch day, and one thing you'll measure that isn't "vibes in the room."
